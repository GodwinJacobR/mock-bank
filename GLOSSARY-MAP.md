# Glossary Map

## Contexts

- [Accounts](./docs/accounts/GLOSSARY.md): customer-facing bank accounts
- [Ledger](./docs/ledger/GLOSSARY.md): records postings against ledger accounts
- [Transfers](./docs/transfers/GLOSSARY.md): moves money between accounts (no terms resolved yet)

## Relationships

- Gateway and web are not domain contexts. They reuse the terms defined above.
- **Accounts ↔ Ledger**: a Bank Account is linked to a Ledger Account by ID only.
